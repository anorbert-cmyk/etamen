Ex
Sanguis

Ex Sanguis is a religious cult that has sworn to destroy all of vampirekind. The group is split into two main disciplines: the Peace provides sustenance and spiritual guidance to all who seek it, while the warlike knights and Inquisitors of the Path hunt down every vampiric whisper, stopping at nothing to cleanse the world of their corruption.

Timeline

2059

Our Dying World

Facing worsening storms and mass migration, a coalition of governments and corporations send FLARE drones into deep space to search for a new home.

2097

Joy from the Dark

A FLARE drone signals home that a distant planet is habitable. In response, governments agree to reserve Earth’s dwindling natural resources for a project known only as “Hope”.

2099

Project HOPE

Project Hope is revealed to the world. It’s a colony ship designed to take a small portion of humanity to this new home. A worldwide drive towards its development and construction is set up, with volunteers entered into a lottery for a berth on the great ship.

2104

Construction Begins Worldwide

The Hope is to be built in modules, with parts launching separately before locking together in Earth’s orbit. She’s scheduled to finish assembly in 2124, and depart in 2126.

2118

Early Success

The Hope’s first module, Australia’s engineering and fabrication module, is successfully launched and placed in geostationary orbit.

2122

Connecting the Pieces

Work now continues aboard the ship, with engineers and scientists working inside its massive hull to connect and test its systems. Departure is now just four years away.

2125

Betrayal

During a now routine passenger loading trip, the Hope’s security forces are overwhelmed by an unknown force of attackers. They seize control of the colony ship, ignite its engines, and pull away from orbit. As the Hope leaves communication range, a woman appears on a video feed broadcasting from the great ship. “I’m not like you, Emperor” she says, snarling and fanged. “I won’t play my part. I won’t obey.”

On Earth, shock turns to grief turns to fury. Pandemonium descends upon the world as citizens turn on each other. Vigilante squads emerge from the chaos, determined to uproot the monsters responsible.

2145

The Purge

For over twenty years, vampires have been hunted relentlessly across the world. These vigilante groups reinterpret religious texts and historical texts to forge an identity of their own. They name themselves Ex Sanguis — from blood — and take up arms with renewed purpose. Through brute force and guile, they put entire vampiric clans and their familiars to death. But Ex Sanguis’ zeal runs deep and wild: in the melee, they murder hundreds of thousands of innocents, too.

In time, the people will come to fear Ex Sanguis just as much as the vampires who inspired their creation.

2177

Today

Over fifty years have passed since the Hope’s disappearance. On the American continent, large parts of the land have become uninhabitable, ravaged by storms and civil unrest that has forced survivors north. Here, the vampiric threat has been largely wiped out, while Ex Sanguis still patrols the lands with their same uncompromising zeal.

Emmy award-winning writer Conor Sheehy, veteran artist Aron Elekes bring the world of Apokrif to life through a fully realized world, with complex characters, compelling storylines, and gorgeous illustrations throughout.

Concept Artist and Illustrator

Aron Elekes

Aron Elekes is a visual developer with fine art roots and over 10 years of experience in game development, a so-called 'veteran' with a vibrant, youthful spirit. He has contributed to a wide spectrum of games and collaborated with leading companies, including Legends of Runeterra, League of Legends, Spellslingers, Mortal Kombat, Riot Games, Netflix, Wizards of the Coast, and Niantic Inc. Beyond his creative endeavors, Aron occasionally believes he can surf (jury's still out on that) and has discovered he can't live without the comfort of palm trees.

Co-Creator and Writer

Conor Sheehy

Conor is a British Emmy award-winning writer with over fifteen years of experience in the games industry. His credits include VALORANT, Arcane, and Legends of Runeterra, where he and Aron first worked together. Outside of writing, Conor is bad at music, mediocre at cooking, and excellent at self deprecation.
