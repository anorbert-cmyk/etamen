The world of Apokrif is dark and fantastical, braiding together elements of history, myth, and science fiction.
Here, human settlements band together in the shadows of old settlements, bracing against catastrophic storms,
vicious packs of animals, and brutal raiders, while the militaristic cult of Ex Sanguis mercilessly hunts down
rumors of vampiric infestations.

Ex
Sanguis

Ex Sanguis is a religious cult founded during the madness of the Purge that has sworn to destroy all of vampirekind. The Peace provides sustenance and spiritual guidance to all who seek it, while the warlike knights and Inquisitors of the Path hunt down every vampiric whisper, stopping at nothing to cleanse the world of their corruption.
